Automatically removes games from your Steam library using AutoHotkey and PowerShell. Uses screen coordinates and OCR to detect the “Remove from account” option, with customizable click delays and hotkeys.<br>
<br>
How to run:<br>
First open your steam app on your PC in windowed fullscreen. Next go to Library. On the left sidebar, open the uncategorized section by pressing on the uncategorized button to make it Fullscreen size library.<br><img width="249" height="154" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7cd15d45-346a-4fba-a85d-a0ccdaad5795" /> 
<br>Lastly open SteamMacro.ahk
<br>
Controls:<br>
F6: Start the macro<br>
F7: Stop and exit<br>
Esc: Exit immediately<br>
<br>
Requirements:<br>
Windows<br>
AutoHotkey v2<br>
PowerShell<br>
Tesseract OCR<br>
<br>
Note: Keep an eye on the macro while it runs. Removing games from your Steam account may be permanent, and some games or licenses may not support removal through the same method.
